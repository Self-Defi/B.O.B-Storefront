# Lesson 07 — First Customer: Evidence and Boundaries

**Case study:** AZHomeInstalls (AHI), October 13, 2026.

## Documented events
- Acquisition source: Facebook Marketplace (FBMP).
- First paid AHI service job; payment reported as **$200 in cash, paid in full**.
- Customer-provided bracket was used to mount an **85-inch TV** (the later owner correction to 85 inches takes precedence over the original 75-inch historical entry).
- Removed **15 small pictures**, left existing picture hooks in the wall at the customer's request, and boxed the pictures.
- Hung a portrait depicting the Last Supper, centered between the living-room windows.
- A courtesy return visit was **planned for the next day at 11 a.m.** for finishing/touch-ups, off the clock. The available record does **not** independently confirm that this visit occurred.
- The owner planned an invoice to email after completing the job. The available record does **not** establish that it was sent.
- Actual before/after project photos are held in the B.O.B. collection repository.

## What these records do not establish
- They do not establish the total number of Facebook Marketplace impressions, conversion rate, ad spend, or profitability of this single job.
- They do not prove the timing of every inquiry, quote, or acceptance step.
- They do not demonstrate that a Stripe payment was used; this job was paid in cash.
- They do not independently confirm that the follow-up visit or the invoice email was completed.

## Educational exercise (not a claim about AHI)
Use fictional names or your own non-sensitive business details to create an intake-to-close checklist: customer inquiry, scoped work, confirmed price, task documentation, payment confirmation, follow-up tracking and record retention. This exercise is recommended training, **not** a reconstruction of all the steps AHI took.

## Reuse and privacy
Before publishing authentic customer photos, confirm publication rights and remove personal details (faces, addresses, metadata, phone numbers and correspondence) as appropriate. Keep customer records out of public repositories.

**B.O.B. rule:** Only claim what the evidence supports. Describe guidance separately from historical actions.
