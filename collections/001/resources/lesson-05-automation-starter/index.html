Outreach & Scheduling Simulator

Practice separate daily-new-send limits, follow-ups, do-not-contact controls, and manual booking review without a real mail server.

Calendar concept

In the real system, busy Google Calendar events must be checked through an authorized integration before offering a time. This sandbox performs no Google Calendar queries and does not make bookings.

Demonstration reflects AHI rules: follow-ups do not count against the new-outbound cap; an unsubscribe or suppression flag blocks contact. These are workflow concepts, not proof of current OAS sending status.
