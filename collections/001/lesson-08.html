Step 1 — Separate startup from ongoing costs

Documented one-time baseline: $116.92. This is two historical domain charges at $10.46 each, $18 for 25 DTF transfers, $18 for four polos, and the reported $60 insurance setup. Heat pressing was free.

Historical monthly baseline: $111. This includes the reported $35 insurance premium, $40 iPostal1, $6 Google Workspace, $10 US Mobile, and a $20 optional ChatGPT allocation. Excluding that optional allocation leaves $91/month in these particular recorded recurring charges.

Correction to earlier notes: a previous summary gave the startup baseline as $124.92. That does not match the itemized figures listed above. Their sum is $116.92; we are not adding an unexplained $8 charge to make the earlier total work.

Step 2 — Understand what the figures cannot prove

  • Domain costs: $10.46 charges were historical and are subject to future renewal; they are not equivalent to a $0 ongoing domain cost.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub: no additional charge was reported for the AHI baseline, but pricing and usage limits may vary.
  • Stripe: transaction-processing charges vary and are not included as a fixed monthly expense.
  • Old subscription trials: Instantly's $47/month plan was a historical tool intended for discontinuation, not part of this ongoing baseline. Cancellation/renewal should be verified independently.
  • Unknown costs: tools, transport, labor time, taxes, extra services, replacements and unrecorded charges are not silently assumed to be zero.
  • Revenue is not profit: the first job brought in $200 cash. We do not have a complete job-level cost ledger establishing its net profit.

Step 6 — Finish Collection 001

  1. Verify or edit each cost in your own worksheet, saving the exported CSV.
  2. Separate upfront purchases, monthly services, yearly renewals, variable fees, and optional subscriptions.
  3. Check your totals and never label a partial ledger “complete.”
  4. Keep receipts, renewal records, contracts and account access under your control.
  5. Ask another beginner to reproduce the figures and explain what remains unknown.

Final B.O.B. standard: a learner can understand the figures, reproduce the calculations, and independently update and maintain the records. We show the real journey from business idea to first paid invoice or payment milestone without claiming undocumented profitability.
