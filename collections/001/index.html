Lesson 01 / Historical timeline

From concept to first revenue

The timeline comes from the AZHomeInstalls historical log updated October 13, 2026. Dates are Phoenix local.

Sep 28, 2026
Business idea and service scope

The residential installation concept took shape; FixtureWorks was replaced by AZHomeInstalls.

Sep 28–30, 2026
Website and intake

Domain and public website, estimate requests, GitHub codebase and Cloudflare services.

Sep 30–Oct 1, 2026
Self-managed CRM

Cloudflare D1, protected admin, quotes, lead status and scheduling.

Oct 1–9, 2026
Business systems

Workspace email, early outbound automation, brand assets and Stripe payment readiness.

Oct 11–12, 2026
Lead signal and setback

First recorded Facebook Marketplace inquiry; Microsoft email test landed in Junk.

Oct 13, 2026
First confirmed payment

A client job was booked and paid: $200 cash collected in full. The client later corrected the television size to 85 inches.

Accuracy matters. Fifteen days is elapsed time from September 28 through October 13, not a guarantee for other businesses. The original October 13 historical log says 75 inches; the owner subsequently corrected the TV size to 85 inches. First revenue does not establish profit.
Real results

One paid customer. One working business foundation.

The first paid service included an 85-inch TV installation, removing and boxing small wall pictures, and hanging a portrait. The original historical document records full cash payment, with the courtesy finishing visit still pending at the time of that report.
